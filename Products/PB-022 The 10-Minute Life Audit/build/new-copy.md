# New copy ledger — PB-022 The 10-Minute Life Audit

Every sentence in the A4 PDF that `spec_coverage.py` does not find in the build
spec, with the reason it is there. One bullet each; the quoted string is the
exact text the gate extracts, so the quotes are generated, not retyped.

**Genuinely new copy in this product is five short pieces of furniture:**

1. The running head `Northing Studio · The 10-Minute Life Audit` on pages 2-4, so
   a printed loose sheet identifies itself.
2. The bottom-centre page numbers `1/4` … `4/4` — ordered by the spec's own
   "Page setup" line ("page numbers bottom-centre in mono, `1/4` style").
3. The mono label `HOW TO SCORE` on page 1, naming the block the spec's page-1
   cell itself calls "How to score:".
4. The spec strip on page 1, required of every product by `brand-kit.md`. Every
   figure in it is measured from the built file.
5. The eight wheel labels, set in caps. The words are the spec's own area
   names; the spec's Assets section orders "labels in Source Sans 3 9 pt".

**One non-copy addition, recorded here at the round-1 critic's request:**

6. **The compass device on page 1.** It carries no words, so no gate sees it, but
   the spec's page-1 layout column orders only "Title block; three short
   paragraphs; a stone box at the base" — no diagram. It is here because
   `brand-kit.md` §"Visual system" makes the four-direction compass the brand's
   signature device ("It appears as an icon, a page diagram and the scoring
   wheel"), and page 1 is the only page in this product where the brand, rather
   than the exercise, is the subject. Round 2 cut it from **88 mm to 52 mm**: at
   88 mm it was a large ringed circle two pages before the 120 mm ringed circle
   the buyer actually scores, and a first-time reader had no way to know the
   first was a brand mark and the second was the task. At 52 mm it reads as a
   mark, not an instrument, and it is no longer sized to fill the sheet — page 1
   is a cover and is declared sparse to the fill gate on that basis, not
   padded to look full.

Everything else below is a **text-extraction artefact**: two or more pieces of
verbatim spec copy sitting on adjacent baselines, which the gate reads as a
single sentence. No product copy has been paraphrased, softened or invented.

There are **no invented facts, statistics, testimonials or research claims** in
this product.
