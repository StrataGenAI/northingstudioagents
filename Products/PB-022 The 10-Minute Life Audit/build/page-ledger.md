**Layout:** title block · three short text blocks · compass device · stone box at the base.

## p.2 — The eight areas

**Purpose:** collect eight honest scores fast.
**Layout:** 8 equal rows sharing the sheet height. Each row: area name (Fraunces 13 pt) · clarifying line (Source Sans 3 9 pt) · 0–10 scale strip in mono, 11 rings to circle · a 1-line "why" field. Alternate rows carry the stone zebra.

| # | Area | Clarifying line (verbatim) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Health | Energy, sleep, movement, how your body feels day to day. |
| 2 | Work | What you spend your working hours on, and whether it's going somewhere. |
| 3 | Money † | How settled you feel about what comes in and goes out. |
| 4 | People | The relationships you'd miss if they went quiet. |
| 5 | Home | The space you live in, and whether it rests you or drains you. |
| 6 | Growth | Learning, skill, the sense of getting better at something. |
| 7 | Play | Fun that isn't productive. Rest that isn't recovery from work. |
| 8 | Direction | Whether your days point somewhere you chose. |

| Element | Exact copy |
|---|---|
| Kicker | `STEP ONE · SCORE` |
| Title | Where things actually are |
| Row prompt (×8) | Why that number? |
| Footnote † | Money here is about how you *feel* about your money, not advice about it. This is not financial advice. |
| Running head **[ADDED]** | `NORTHING STUDIO` · `THE 10-MINUTE LIFE AUDIT` |
| Page number **[ADDED]** | `2/4` |

**Writing space:** 8 × one ruled "why" line at the **7.5 mm** brand-kit pitch, on
all three editions, plus 88 rings to circle. **Built: 8.**

## p.3 — Your wheel

**Layout:** eight-spoke wheel centred, **120 mm across on A4** · spokes labelled · 5 hairline stone rings at 2/4/6/8/10 · tick marks at 1/3/5/7/9 · **scale numbers 2/4/6/8/10 repeated in all eight wedges**, set just inside the ring they label so the rings stay unbroken · instruction strip below the wheel · three short fields · closing italic line.

|---|---|
| Kicker | `STEP TWO · SEE IT` |
| Title | Your wheel |
| Instruction | Mark each score on its spoke, then join the marks. Shade it in if you like. The shape matters more than the numbers — you're looking for the dents. |

## p.4 — One small move + what's next

**Purpose:** convert the picture into a single action, then offer the depth honestly.
**Layout:** an intro band — left = the action block's kicker, title and body; right = the quiet stone panel **at its natural height** — then the five writing fields at **full sheet width** beneath it. Round 2 change: in round 1 the panel was stretched to full column height around a spacer, which left a 173.3 mm void inside it.

| Element | Exact copy |
|---|---|
| Kicker | `STEP THREE · MOVE` |
| Title | One small move |
| Body | Take your lowest area — or the one that surprised you, which is often the real answer. Don't fix it. Just make one move this week. |
| Field 1 | The area I'm picking: |
| Field 2 | One move I'll make this week: |
| Field 3 | When, exactly: |
| Field 4 | How I'll know I did it: |
| Field 5 | Re-score in 30 days. Date to re-score: — set as the instruction sentence in sans, on its own line, above `DATE TO RE-SCORE:` in mono. Same words, two objects. |
| Panel kicker | `IF YOU WANT MORE` |
| Panel body | This audit gives you the picture. **The Balance Audit** gives you the rest of the method: guided questions for all eight areas so the scores are honest, a gap map, three standing rules, a 30-day plan, and twelve monthly re-scores so you can watch the shape change. |
| Honest limit | This free audit is complete as it is — the paid one goes deeper, it doesn't unlock this. |
| Footer disclaimer | This is a structured self-reflection tool. It is not a diagnosis, an assessment or therapy. If something here worries you, talk to someone qualified — that is a strong move, not a weak one. |
