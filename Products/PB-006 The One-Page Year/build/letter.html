The One-Page Year

One page you'll actually look at.

Fill this in once. Put it where you'll see it. Come back to it on the first Sunday of every month.

1The sentence

If this year went well, what would be true by the end of it? Write one sentence.

2Three goals

Three things, not ten. Number them in the order you'd protect them if the year got hard.

1. 2. 3.

3Why these

For each goal, finish this line: This matters because…

1. 2. 3.

4The first step

The smallest next step for each goal — something you could do in under an hour.

1. 2. 3.

5The floor

One habit you'll keep even on your worst week. Write the smallest version of it that still counts.

A structured self-reflection tool. Not therapy, diagnosis or financial advice.

HOW TO USE

Five minutes now,
five minutes a month

  1. 1

    Print it, or open it in your tablet notes app.

  2. 2

    Answer the five blocks in one sitting. Don't polish — first answers are usually the honest ones.

  3. 3

    Put it somewhere you pass every day.

  4. 4

    On the first Sunday of each month, read it and change anything that's no longer true.

  5. 5

    Cross things out. A plan you've edited is a plan you're using.
