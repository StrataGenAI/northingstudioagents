# Brand kit — Northing Studio (v2, 2026-09-12)

> **Status: the name is decided.** The owner chose **Northing Studio** on 2026-09-12 after "Quiet Compass" proved unavailable on Etsy, has registered `northingstudio.gumroad.com`, and will claim the Etsy shop name directly. Its conflict screen returned **CONFLICT** and the owner reviewed and accepted that risk knowingly — the findings are in the screen table below, and a professional trademark search remains outstanding. The palette, type, voice and page system were built to stand independently of the name and are unchanged.

## Positioning

For **thoughtful people who want their days to point somewhere** — not another tracker — **Northing Studio** is the **guided, warm, printable-and-tablet self-development studio** that gives you a method (audit → direction → 1% days → review), shows you every page before you buy, and works on paper, on a tablet and in Notion.

**Evidence:** the report's positioning map puts *warm, light, editorial × guided method* as the only open quadrant, occupied solely by Ideas To Thrive, which has thin proof (9 ratings on its $49 flagship) — report §"Branding & positioning map"; `research/sellers/gumroad-S3-ideastothrive.md` §F. Guided-method PDFs are proven on Gumroad: *Live Intentionally* 13,089 × $19.99 and *The M.E.A.N. Workbook* 4,475 × $47 — report §"Best-seller teardown". 12 of 15 flagship thumbnails carry a Notion logo, a device and grey/black/blue sans type — `research/audits/thumbs-gumroad-top.png`.

## Name

**Chosen: Northing Studio** — the owner's decision, 2026-09-12. "Northing" is a navigation term, the distance travelled north, so the compass device, the palette and the whole page system survive the change unaltered.

**Superseded: "Quiet Compass"** — the name this kit was originally built around, and the name the conflict screen below actually examined. The owner found it **unavailable on Etsy** on 2026-09-12. The screen table below was later run against "Northing Studio" as well — see its row.

Merged from the report's directions: the **palette and compass device** come from the "Quiet Compass" direction; the **editorial page craft** (letter-spaced kickers, spec strips, worked examples, one framework per page) comes from "Field Manual"; the **method** (floor habits, restart protocol, 1% loop) comes from "1% Studio". Kept as one brand rather than three, because the research shows consistency across the shop grid is what wins (Easlo: 24,310 ratings on a single monochrome cover formula — report §"Seller profiles 05").

**Why not the other two:**
- **"1% Studio" — rejected on conflict.** `1percnt.com` (multimedia production studio), `onepercentstudio.com` (art/invention studio, 30 years), and **The 1% Planner** (`theonepercentplanner.com`), an existing undated goal planner — a direct category neighbour. Three live users of the name, one in our exact category.
- **"Field Manual" — rejected as a brand, kept as a product-line word.** It is a generic descriptor with no ownable identity, and Ideas To Thrive already subtitles its flagship *"A working manual"* (`research/assets/gumroad/ideastothrive/discipline-system/01-cover.png`). Using it as our house name would read as a follower. We use "manual" only as a product descriptor.

**Shortlist screened (2026-09-12):** Quiet Compass · 1% Studio · Field Manual · Life Compass · Balance Wheel. **"Northing Studio" was not in this shortlist** — it was screened separately later the same day; its row heads the table below.

## Palette

Measured against the competitor palettes in the report §"Palettes on Gumroad": cool greys and dark mode dominate, and **warm paper + deep ink + one confident accent is almost unused**.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Paper / background | `#FBF8F3` | page and listing backgrounds; the default ground for every product and every listing image |
| Ink / text | `#1F2A36` | all body text, prompts, rules, writing lines. The only colour allowed for small text |
| Primary | `#3E6B89` | headings, tabs, section kickers, diagram strokes, the compass device |
| Accent | `#D9A441` | highlights, badges, the "you are here" marker, one focal element per image. **Never for text.** Max ~10% of any image |
| Support | `#7C9A7E` | Kaizen/growth marks, floor-habit chips, secondary fills, charts |
| Stone (neutral fill) | `#E4DCD0` | worked-example boxes, table zebra rows, quiet panels |
| **Print-friendly variant** | ink `#1F2A36` at 100%, primary at 60% tint, **no full-bleed fills** | Every printable ships an ink-light build: white ground, hairline `0.5 pt` rules, tints instead of solids, target **≤8% ink coverage**. Evidence: Ideas To Thrive markets "8% ink coverage" as a feature (`research/sellers/gumroad-S3-ideastothrive.md` §C6) |

**Contrast / accessibility rules**
- Body text is always ink `#1F2A36` on paper `#FBF8F3` (very high contrast, passes AA comfortably).
- Primary `#3E6B89` on paper is for headings and large text (≥18 pt) and UI labels only — not for 9–11 pt body.
- Accent `#D9A441` never carries text or meaning on its own; it is always paired with a label or shape, so the page still reads in greyscale and for colour-blind users.
- Every product must print legibly in **pure greyscale** — this is a QA gate, not a preference.

## Typography (commercial-use licensed)

All three are **SIL Open Font License 1.1**, verified 2026-09-12. OFL permits commercial use and PDF embedding; the font files may never be sold on their own, and the licence text ships in our `/licences` folder with every product.

| Role | Font | Licence (verified) | Sizes (print) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headings / display | **Fraunces** (variable; use the `SOFT`/`WONK` axes sparingly) | OFL 1.1 — `github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/fraunces/OFL.txt`, verified 2026-09-12 | Page title 28–34 pt · section 18–22 pt · italic accent line 13–15 pt |
| Body / prompts | **Source Sans 3** | OFL 1.1 — `github.com/adobe-fonts/source-sans`, verified 2026-09-12 | Body 10–11 pt · prompts 10.5 pt · captions 9 pt (**never below 9 pt**) |
| Numerals / labels / trackers | **IBM Plex Mono** | OFL 1.1 — `github.com/IBM/plex`, verified 2026-09-12 | Kickers 8 pt / 12% letter-spacing, caps · scores and dates 10–14 pt · spec strips 8.5 pt |

Backup serif if Fraunces ever proves awkward at small sizes: **Source Serif 4** (OFL 1.1, verified same day). No other font may enter a product without a licence check recorded in this file.

**Pairing rule:** Fraunces for the *statement* (titles, the one italic accent line), Source Sans 3 for everything the buyer has to read or write on, IBM Plex Mono for anything that is a number, a code or a label. Never more than three type sizes on a listing image.

## Voice

**Three adjectives: calm · specific · kind-but-firm.**

Anti-model from the evidence: STRONGLAND's male-coded, shaming copy with weight-loss claims (`research/audits/gumroad-lifemathmoney-tough.md` §"Weaknesses"), and The Overwhelmed Brain's diagnostic-sounding promises with no disclaimer (`research/audits/gumroad-theoverwhelmedbrain-jkSo.md`).

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Twenty minutes a day, for seven days." | "Transform your entire life in one week!" |
| "Pick the one thing you'd keep if you could only keep one." | "Crush your goals and dominate your year." |
| "A bad week is data, not failure. Here's the restart page." | "No excuses. Most people quit here." |
| "This is paper and PDF. You do the thinking; the pages hold the structure." | "This system does the work for you." |
| "For reflection and planning. Not therapy, diagnosis or financial advice." | "Find out if you're burnt out / what's wrong with you." |
| Name the mechanism: "the Floor", "the Restart Page", "the Vital Few", "the Standing Rules" | Borrowed method names: GTD, PARA, 12 Week Year, WOOP, Wheel of Life, Atomic Habits |
| Second person, present tense, short declarative sentences | Emoji-stacked hype, fake urgency, "Limited time" that never expires |

**Always-on disclaimers.** Money products: *"Educational only — not financial advice."* Audit and reflection products: *"A structured self-reflection tool, not a diagnosis, assessment or therapy."* These appear on the listing, on the product's "Start here" page, and in the footer of any scoring page.

## Visual system

- **Logo / wordmark:** "NORTHING STUDIO" set in Fraunces, letter-spaced small caps, ink on paper. No icon lock-up needed at small sizes — the wordmark alone is the signature.
- **Signature device — the four-direction compass.** Our own drawing: a simple circle, four points, one brass "you are here" marker. **N** Direction (purpose/values) · **E** Energy (what feeds you) · **S** Systems (habits and structure) · **W** Wellbeing (balance and rest). It appears as an icon, a page diagram and the scoring wheel. **It is never the four-circle ikigai Venn** — that diagram is borrowed and widely copied (`research/audits/gumroad-anferneeck-my-ikigai.md` §"IP flags").
- **Second device — the Kaizen loop.** A single continuous arrow loop with three notches: *Floor · Ideal · Restart*. Our drawing, used across PB-004 and the 1% pages.
- **Icon style:** 1.25 pt stroke, rounded caps, no fills, ink or primary only, drawn on a 24 px grid. One icon per product family, never more than one icon per listing image.
- **Line weights:** hairline rules `0.5 pt` stone; section rules `1 pt` primary; diagram strokes `1.25 pt`.
- **Corner radius:** 2 mm on boxes and chips. Nothing fully rounded, nothing sharp.
- **Texture:** none. Flat warm paper. No paper photos, no shadows on page backgrounds. Soft drop shadows are allowed *only* on mockup page stacks in listing images.
- **Writing space:** ruled lines at **7.5 mm** (print) with a 0.5 pt stone rule. Blank answer boxes get a 2 mm radius stone outline, never a grey fill (it wastes ink and reads badly when scanned).

## Listing image rules

Derived from the report's 10-slot blueprint and the thumbnail test (`research/audits/thumbs-gumroad-top.png`).

**Thumbnail formula we will own (square, Gumroad Discover grid):**
> warm paper ground `#FBF8F3` → letter-spaced mono kicker (`NORTHING STUDIO`) → **one Fraunces benefit line of ≤6 words** → one line-art device (compass / wheel / loop / 80-20 bars) in primary with a single brass marker → a thin spec strip in mono at the base.

No device mockup on the thumbnail, no logo of any third-party app, no busy desk scene. Rationale: 11 of 15 competitor thumbnails show a device and 12 of 15 show a Notion badge; **only one warm, light, editorial thumbnail exists in the whole competitor set**, so this composition is instantly distinguishable at 220 px.

- **Headline rules:** ≤6 words, one promise, readable at 220 px. Benefit over product name (Easlo's principle). Never stack more than one headline per image.
- **Hero (16:9, 1280×720):** the benefit line left, the product shown right as a **flat page stack** (2–3 pages, slight tilt, soft shadow) or a tablet — never both in the same frame.
- **Mockup style:** flat page stacks and clipboard/desk flat-lays for printables; one tablet-portrait frame for the hyperlinked edition; a paper-and-pen "in use" shot for the DEMO slot. No hands holding devices in the hero; no 3D box renders (that is STRONGLAND's and Daniel Marthi's convention).
- **Spec strip** (mono, 8.5 pt, on every INCL image): `24 PAGES · 7 DAYS · A4 + LETTER · PRINT + TABLET · UNDATED`.
- **Grid consistency:** same ground, same kicker position, same type sizes on every listing in the shop; **one accent colour per product family** (Focus = brass, Direction = primary blue, Kaizen = sage, Reflection = stone) so the shop grid reads as one brand but each product is findable.
- **Every product ships all 10 slots.** The research shows flagships at $49–$149 with 1–3 images; a complete tour is our cheapest advantage.
- **Honesty rules:** no "Verified by Gumroad"-style badges implying platform endorsement, no struck-through prices that were never charged, no proof numbers until we actually have them, no stale year labels.
