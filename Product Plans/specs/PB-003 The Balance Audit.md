# Build spec — PB-003 The Balance Audit (v1)

Brief: `2026-09-12 Product Plan.md` §"Launch product briefs" → PB-003 · Brand kit: `brand-kit.md` v1 · Owner decisions pending: brand name; Notion auto-radar tier at launch or later

**What it is:** a 20-page visual self-audit — eight areas scored with guided questions, drawn as a big legible wheel, turned into a gap map, three standing rules and a 30-day plan, then re-scored monthly for a year. **$12 print+tablet / $19 with the Notion tier.**

**Accent colour for this family:** primary blue `#3E6B89` with brass markers.

> **Compliance, non-negotiable.** Never use "Wheel of Life" in the name, title, tags, file names, headers, image text or alt text. **WHEEL OF LIFE® is a live US registration (Reg. 3918518, Ser. 77779648, Success Motivation International, Inc.) covering books, booklets, manuals and printed instructional material for personal development, goal setting and planning** — our exact goods. Our language: "the balance wheel diagram", "the eight-area wheel", "your wheel". The product is **The Balance Audit**.
>
> **Non-clinical rule.** This product scores satisfaction and attention, never symptoms. No "burnout", "anxiety", "depression", "diagnosis", "assessment" or "healing" language anywhere — that is the lane The Overwhelmed Brain occupies without disclaimers, and we are deliberately not following it.

## Hyperlink / tab map (digital)

- **Tab rail** right edge: `START · AREAS · WHEEL · GAPS · THREE · RULES · 30 DAYS · RE-SCORE`.
- The eight area pages are linked in a ring (prev/next) and each links to the wheel page.
- The wheel page (p.12) carries eight small hotspots — tapping a spoke label jumps back to that area's page. The gap map (p.13) does the same per row.
- p.14 links to the three chosen areas' pages.
- Every page → tab rail → any section in one tap. Every page has a back chevron.
- Outbound: p.20 → PB-002, PB-005 listings.

## Notion tier (the $19 option)

Same eight areas, same questions, plus what paper can't do: scores entered once auto-draw the radar; monthly re-scores stack into a trend chart; the gap map is a linked database view sorted by gap size; standing rules live on the dashboard where they're visible.
