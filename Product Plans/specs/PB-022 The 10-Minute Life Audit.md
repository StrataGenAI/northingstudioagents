Brief: `2026-09-12 Product Plan.md` §"Launch product briefs" → PB-022 · Brand kit: `brand-kit.md` v1 · Owner decisions pending: brand name confirmation; free-listing structure (plan §"Open questions" Q3)

**What it is:** a free, 4-page scored audit that gives the reader a number and one next step. It is the funnel into PB-003, and it is the first place anyone sees our eight-area wheel drawn properly.

> **Compliance, non-negotiable:** never use the phrase "Wheel of Life" anywhere in this product, its file names, its listing, its tags or its image text. **WHEEL OF LIFE® is a live US registration (Reg. 3918518, Success Motivation International, Inc.) covering printed personal-development, goal-setting and planning materials.** Our device is "the eight-area wheel" or "the balance wheel diagram", and the product is "The 10-Minute Life Audit".

## Page setup

Margins 15 mm · 2-column grid, 6 mm gutter · scoring rows on a 9 mm baseline · Fraunces titles, Source Sans 3 prompts, IBM Plex Mono for all numbers and scale labels · paper `#FBF8F3`, ink `#1F2A36`, primary `#3E6B89` (wheel strokes), accent `#D9A441` (the single "lowest area" marker), stone `#E4DCD0` (score-row zebra) · page numbers bottom-centre in mono, `1/4` style
