## Page setup

Margins 15 mm (top 18 mm) · single column with a 3-column inner grid, 6 mm gutters · writing lines 7.5 mm, 0.5 pt `#E4DCD0` · Fraunces titles, Source Sans 3 body/prompts, IBM Plex Mono kickers and numbers · ink `#1F2A36`, primary `#3E6B89`, accent `#D9A441`, stone `#E4DCD0` · footer left: `NORTHING STUDIO` wordmark; footer right: `northingstudio.example / v1.0 · 2026-09` · no page numbers (2 pages)

- [ ] Fonts embedded (Fraunces, Source Sans 3, IBM Plex Mono); text readable at 100% print
- [ ] Opens and annotates correctly in GoodNotes and Notability (and Samsung Notes if claimed)
- [ ] A4 and Letter both correct; nothing clipped inside margins; Letter is a genuine re-layout
- [ ] Prints cleanly in pure greyscale; ink coverage ≤8%
- [ ] Writing lines are 7.5 mm and actually writable with a normal pen
- [ ] File is a free/$0 product under Gumroad's 250 MB cap; named consistently
- [ ] Copy proofread; no competitor wording; **no "Wheel of Life", no GTD/PARA/12 Week Year/Atomic Habits/WOOP**; disclaimer present
- [ ] Outbound link to PB-002 works and is not a tracking-heavy URL
