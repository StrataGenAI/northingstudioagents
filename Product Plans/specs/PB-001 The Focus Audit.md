**What it is:** a 24-page, 7-day guided sprint that finds the 20% of a person's time, energy, tasks and inputs that actually moves their life — and ends with a one-page Focus Charter. **$9.** The lowest-competition category we found: no named Pareto/focus-audit product exists on Gumroad.

## Page setup

Margins 15 mm, top 18 mm · 3-column grid, 6 mm gutters · writing lines 7.5 mm, 0.5 pt stone · log tables on a 8 mm row · Fraunces titles 26 pt / section heads 17 pt / italic accent 13 pt · Source Sans 3 body 10.5 pt, prompts 10.5 pt, captions 9 pt · IBM Plex Mono kickers 8 pt caps 12% tracked, all numbers · ink `#1F2A36`, primary `#3E6B89`, brass `#D9A441`, sage `#7C9A7E`, stone `#E4DCD0` · running header: day number left in mono, `THE FOCUS AUDIT` right · page numbers bottom-centre mono

**Repeating page furniture:** every Day page opens with a mono kicker `DAY n · <NAME>`, a one-line time cost ("About 20 minutes"), and closes with a single brass-ruled "Today's one line" strip so each day produces one sentence the Charter can use.

## Hyperlink / tab map (digital)

- **Tab rail** down the right edge of every page, 7 tabs in mono 8 pt: `MAP · 1 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6 · 7 · WEEKS · END`. Current tab in brass, rest in stone.
- Every page: tab rail → that section's first page. Every page footer: a small "← back to map" chevron.
- Day pages link forward to the next day and back to the previous.
- p. 13 carries four "pull forward" markers linking back to pages 6, 8, 10 and 12 so the reader can re-read their one-liners.
- Outbound: p. 24 → PB-002 and PB-004 listings.
- **Rule:** every page must be reachable from the map in one tap, and every page must have a way back.
