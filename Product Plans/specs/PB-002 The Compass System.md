# Build spec — PB-002 The Compass System (v1)

Brief: `2026-09-12 Product Plan.md` §"Launch product briefs" → PB-002 · Brand kit: `brand-kit.md` v1 · Owner decisions pending: brand name; Notion tier at launch or later; whether the $29 tier is "+ Notion" or "+ Notion + setup call"

**What it is:** the flagship. A 60-page system that runs a full year: values → our own four-direction compass with a scoring wheel → a one-page vision → quarterly goal sheets → 90-day roadmaps → a 1% daily log → weekly and quarterly reviews → a letter to your future self. **$19 core / $29 with the Notion tier.**

**Renamed from "Life Compass OS"** — "Life Compass" is crowded on Etsy and Gumroad *and* is the name of an ACT (therapy) values worksheet, which collides with our non-clinical rule. Brand kit §Name records the screen.

**Accent colour for this family:** primary blue `#3E6B89`.

## Page setup

Margins 15 mm, top 18 mm · 3-column grid, 6 mm gutters · writing lines 7.5 mm · Fraunces titles 28 pt / section 18 pt / italic accent 13 pt · Source Sans 3 body 10.5 pt · IBM Plex Mono kickers, numbers, dates · full brand palette; **one section colour per part** (Part 1 sage, Part 2 primary, Part 3 brass, Part 4 stone) applied only to kickers and rules, never to fills · running header: part name left, `THE COMPASS SYSTEM` right · page numbers bottom-centre

**Structure:** four parts. Part 1 Direction (pp. 3–18) · Part 2 Goals (pp. 19–30) · Part 3 Days (pp. 31–46) · Part 4 Review (pp. 47–60).

## Hyperlink / tab map (digital)

- **Tab rail** right edge, four part tabs + sub-tabs: `1 DIRECTION` (values · compass · vision) · `2 GOALS` (Q1–Q4) · `3 DAYS` (design · log M1–M12 · restart) · `4 REVIEW` (W1–W13 · quarter).
- The compass page (p.9) links from each arm to that direction's page (pp.10–13).
- Each quarterly goal sheet links to its roadmap; each roadmap links back.
- Each month log page links forward/back and to the restart page.
- Weekly review pages run in a ring W1→W13 and each links to the current quarter's goal sheet.
- The restart page is reachable **from every page in Part 3** — that is the point of it.
- Outbound: p.60 → PB-004, PB-005.

## Notion tier (the $29 option)

The compass scores auto-draw; the 1% log is a database with a three-state select and a rolling floor/ideal count; goals link to directions as a relation; weekly reviews pull that week's log automatically; the restart page is a button that resets one habit. Delivered as a duplicate link plus a 6-minute setup video. **"Works with Notion" as plain text only — no logo, no badge.** Keep the video current: the research shows stale tutorials are a recurring buyer complaint.

## Assets

- **Four-direction compass** master (our own; N/E/S/W as Direction/Energy/Systems/Wellbeing) — icon, full diagram, four-arm scored version. **Never the ikigai Venn.**
- **Kaizen loop** (Floor → Ideal → Restart)
- Four part-divider devices; value-bank typesetting (120 words, 4 columns)
- Three-state log glyphs (● ◐ ○) drawn as vectors, not font characters, so they survive any PDF reader
- Listing images: compass anatomy with callouts, the system arc (values → compass → goals → 1% → review), the 1% log page, the restart page (this is the emotional hook — show it), tiers comparison, formats, page-count grid
- No stock photography; no third-party logos
