# Build spec — PB-005 The Review Ritual (v1)

Brief: `2026-09-12 Product Plan.md` §"Launch product briefs" → PB-005 · Brand kit: `brand-kit.md` v1 · Owner decisions pending: brand name; Notion tier at launch or later · **Build order: last of the seven** (wave 2, weeks 9–11)

**What it is:** an 80-page undated year of reviews — 52 weekly, 12 monthly, 4 quarterly, 1 annual — built as a **cascade**, where each level reads the level below it. **$15.** It exists for retention: it is the product that brings a buyer back to the brand every Sunday.

**Accent colour for this family:** stone `#E4DCD0` with primary blue.

## Page setup

Margins 15 mm · 2-column grid on review pages, 6 mm gutter · writing lines 7.5 mm · Fraunces titles 24 pt / section 16 pt / italic accent 12 pt · Source Sans 3 prompts 10.5 pt · IBM Plex Mono for week numbers, dates and counts · running header: level name left, `THE REVIEW RITUAL` right · **undated throughout** — week numbers, not dates, with a blank date field on every page

**Design constraint:** 52 near-identical pages is the risk here. Solve it with a fixed grid and a single varying element — each week carries **one rotating "question of the week"** from a set of 52 original prompts, so the ritual doesn't go stale. That one line is the product's whole differentiation from a free reflection template.

## Hyperlink / tab map (digital)

- **Tab rail:** `START · WEEKS · MONTHS · QUARTERS · YEAR`, with the weeks tab opening a **week index page** (a 52-cell grid, each cell tapping through to that week) — at 52 pages a linear rail alone is unusable.
- Cascade links, both directions: each weekly page → its month; each monthly page → its four weeks **and** its quarter; each quarterly page → its three months and the annual pages.
- Weekly pages run in a ring W1 ⇄ W52.
- Outbound: p.80 → PB-002, PB-003.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Fonts embedded; 80 pages proofread; page count matches the listing claim
- [ ] **All 52 rotating questions are unique, original and non-repeating** — read them in sequence and check no two months feel the same
- [ ] Week index: all 52 cells link to the right page; cascade links work in both directions
- [ ] All links tested in GoodNotes + Notability (and Samsung Notes if claimed)
- [ ] Undated throughout — no month names, no year, no weekday assumptions
- [ ] A4 and Letter separately laid out; ≤8% ink coverage; greyscale legible
- [ ] Money question carries the "not financial advice" footnote; disclaimer on p. 2 and p. 80
- [ ] Copy proofread; no competitor wording; no third-party method names or trademarks
- [ ] The weekly page is genuinely writable in 20 minutes — time yourself doing one before shipping
